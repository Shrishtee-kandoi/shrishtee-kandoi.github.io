Shrishtee Kandoi

Bioinformatician / Data Engineer · Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai

New York, NY

Hello there, I’m Shrishtee! I am a bioinformatician and data engineer at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, where I’m learning all the cool tricks to decode neurodegenerative diseases.

I’m interested in anything that involves genes, brains, and all things fun. My recent work includes genome-wide and transcriptomic studies of progressive supranuclear palsy (PSP).

The best way to reach me is by email at shrishtee.kandoi@mssm.edu.
